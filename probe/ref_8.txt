			<div class="faq-view-title">
				Data privacy
			</div>
	
			<div class="faq-view-meta">
				<i class="far fa-clock mr-1"></i>
				Last updated on
				07/16/2026 08:01 PM UTC
			</div>
		</div>
	
		<div class="card-body faq-view-body">
			<div class="faq-article-content">
				
					<p>At SWGT, protecting your data and privacy is one of our highest priorities. Every major feature is designed with security, privacy, and account isolation in mind from the beginning&mdash;not as an afterthought.<br><br></p><p><b>What information does SWGT collect?</b></p><ul><li>Information required to create and manage your SWGT account, such as your email address and account preferences.</li><li>Game data that you explicitly choose to upload or synchronize through SWEX or the SWGT plugins.</li><li>Guild information, friend relationships, and other data necessary to provide the features you enable.</li><li>Basic technical information required to operate and secure the platform, such as authentication sessions and security logs.</li></ul><p>SWGT only collects the information necessary to provide its services and does not sell your personal information.<br><br></p><p><b>About the SWGT web platform and web services</b></p><ul><li>Each SWGT site is architecturally isolated and designed to access only its own database. By design, one SWGT site cannot directly access another site's data.
